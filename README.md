Swimming Pool Management System
Each client has a dashboard/profile:
- check the status of their pool and things like the temperature of the water
- Requests services like backwash or adding extra chemicals
- Request Cleaning staff
Push Notifications whenever a change occurs on the client pool

  Cleaning staff leaves notes based on status
This system will be beneficial for both the client and the business:
The business:
-will have all the clients on the app
-as well as staff
-cleaning staff will update client after each visit and what they've done during the visit

System may Recommend certain actions to be taken based on the current <i>Health</i> of the pool.
