Simulating Location in Android Studio
You can't simulate the actual process of a user granting permission, but you can easily simulate the location data the device returns, which is the most important part for testing data fetching.

1. Simulating Permission Status
Since you cannot simulate the permission dialog itself, you must manually control the permission state for testing the different branches of your code:

Test Permission Granted: Grant the location permission manually in your test device/emulator settings before launching the app.

Test Permission Denied: Deny the location permission in your test device/emulator settings before launching the app.

2. Simulating Geographic Location (Coordinates)
You can feed fake GPS coordinates to your emulator/test device to simulate the user being anywhere in the world. This is crucial for testing different weather conditions and geocoding results.

A. Using the Extended Controls
While your app is running on the Android Emulator, click the three dots (...) icon on the emulator toolbar to open the Extended Controls window.

Navigate to the Location section (the pin icon).

You can manually enter Latitude and Longitude values (e.g., for Miami, FL: Lat 25.76, Lng -80.19).

Click the SET LOCATION button.

Your app will now receive this simulated location when it calls LocationServices.getFusedLocationProviderClient().getLastLocation().

B. Using Google Maps in the Emulator
Open the Maps app inside the Android Emulator.

Find a specific location (e.g., a pool store, a specific city).

Press and hold on the map to drop a pin.

The emulator will often automatically send the coordinates of the dropped pin to the system's location service, which your app will then use.

C. Using a GPS Exchange Format (GPX) File
For testing moving scenarios (like a user driving), you can load a GPX file containing a sequence of coordinates:

In the Extended Controls window, go to the Location tab.

In the Playback section, click Load GPX.

Load a prepared GPX file.

Click Play to simulate the device "moving" along that route.
